❓ How to use SnackScout

Short how-tos for everything in SnackScout. Press Ctrl+K anywhere to jump to any page or setting.

📝 Scout a match

You can scout any match, ours or anyone else's, and notes from every scout sync to the whole team.

  1. Open the competition: 🏆 Competitions → pick the event (or Quick actions → Today's competition).
  2. Find the match on ⭐ Our matches or 🗓 All matches, and press 📝 Scout.
  3. The match window opens with the video on the left. It starts at the right moment. Fill in the form on the right, while it plays or afterwards.
  4. Switch robots with the tabs at the top (🔴/🔵 team numbers). Each robot has its own form. Press 💾 Save or Ctrl+Enter.
  5. Use 🗒 Match notes for the whole match: what happened, what to change next time.

Also on team pages: open a team, scroll to Matches, and press 📝 Scout on a row.

🏆 Competition day

  • Next match card: countdown, partners and opponents, the predicted score, and the top strategy tips. 🗒 Plan / notes saves your plan.
  • ⭐ Our matches: every match has a prediction and strategy tips. Upcoming ones show their tips open.
  • 🗓 All matches: the whole schedule with scores, predictions, upsets 🔥 and "what to watch". Type a team number to find all its matches.
  • 🏅 Rankings: live from the official scoring system. The top 4 are likely alliance captains.
  • Event highs: high score, best auto, biggest win, closest match and upsets so far, at the top of the page.
  • Viewing as team: type any team number to see the schedule and strategy from their side.

The page refreshes on its own during an event, and pauses while you're typing or have a window open.

🤝 Alliance selection

  1. Open the competition → 🤝 Alliance selection.
  2. The suggestions rank every team by fit: OPR, auto and park reliability, an auto stronger than ours, penalties, and breakdowns our scouts saw. The reasons are listed so you can check them.
  3. Press ＋ to add a team to the pick list. Reorder with ↑ ↓.
  4. During selection, mark teams picked by others, declined us or don't pick. Everyone on the team sees the same list.

👥 Look up any team

  • Type a number, a name, a competition, a city or an event code in the search bar (/). It searches teams and competitions worldwide.
  • Team pages show summary tags, OPR and rank, every match with video, our scouting, and pit notes.
  • Press ☆ to favorite a team. Favorites and recently viewed teams show on the home page.
  • ⚖️ Compare up to 6 teams side by side. Type 34672, 16750 in the search bar as a shortcut.

🎬 Videos & OpenCV

  • ▶ Watch opens the match inside SnackScout, starting at the right moment (YouTube's own page sometimes resumes where you last stopped).
  • ⬇ on a team page downloads the match so it plays without Wi-Fi (about 2 minutes).
  • 🔬 Analyze (in a team page's Scout panel) reads the scoreboard and spots pollen shots. Mark the two hives once per livestream, then tap Ours / Partner / Not a shot on each burst.
  • Wrong moment or wrong match? On a team page, open Video settings under the match table and press 🔍 Auto-sync. At two-field events, SnackScout picks the stream whose title names the match's field.

💬 Slack

  • /scout 16750 for a team, /scout next for our next match, /scout event for today's schedule and results, /scout compare 34672 16750.
  • #snackscout gets match reminders about 15 minutes before our matches, results after each match, and new scouting notes.
  • Slack only works while SnackScout runs on the team laptop. Admins set it up under Settings → Slack.

🌐 Sharing & the launcher

  • On the team laptop, open SnackScout Launcher (Desktop or Start menu) and press 🌐 Go online. Teammates open kieran-snack.github.io/scout, the same link every time, and the QR code works too.
  • Copy invite for team copies the link and the team code for your team chat.
  • ↻ Restart reloads SnackScout after an update. The online link stays up.
  • New teammates: Continue with Google on the login page, then enter the team code. No password needed.

⚙️ Settings

  • Look & feel: 8 color palettes or your own color, dark mode, text size, animations.
  • Scouting: how often event pages refresh, and whether match tables show every column.
  • Account: your name, password, and Google sign-in.
  • Team cloud: sync status. Scouting saved offline uploads later.
