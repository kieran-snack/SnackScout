Where does the data come from?
  • FTCScout + FTC Events API: official scores, ranks, OPR, and each robot's auto leave/park and endgame park.
  • YouTube (yt-dlp): finds each event's livestream and jumps to every match. Clips can be downloaded to watch without Wi-Fi.
  • OpenCV: reads the scoreboard in match videos and spots pollen shots.
  • Your scouts: per-robot pollen counts come from the Scout form on each match. Official data only counts per alliance.
